Privacy Policy

Serenity Mindspace ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform. Mental health information is among the most sensitive data there is, and we treat it accordingly.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you register, we collect your name, email address, user type (user or specialist), and password hash. We never store plain-text passwords.

Wellness Assessment Data

If you complete our wellness assessment, we store your responses to support personalised recommendations. This includes mood ratings, mental health history, daily functioning indicators, and goals.

Session Data

Text and video sessions are logged with timestamps, duration, and session type. Chat transcripts, if saved, are encrypted at rest.

Specialist Credentials

Specialists submit government ID, degree certificates, and practising licences for verification. These documents are stored securely and reviewed only by our authorised admin team.

Payment Information

Token purchases are processed by third-party payment providers (Razorpay/Stripe). We do not store card numbers or CVVs.

Technical Data

We may collect your IP address, browser type, device information, and usage patterns for security and product improvement.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing, operating, and improving the platform
  • Matching you with appropriate specialists based on assessment data
  • Processing token transactions
  • Sending appointment reminders and account notifications
  • Detecting and responding to crisis situations
  • Verifying specialist credentials
  • Complying with legal obligations

We will never sell your personal data to third parties for advertising purposes.

3. Health Information & Special Protections

Mental health information is classified as sensitive personal data under applicable law. We apply additional protections:

  • Assessment data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Chat transcripts are accessible only to you and your chosen specialist
  • Health data is never used for advertising profiling
  • You may delete your health data at any time from your profile settings
  • Specialist credentials are reviewed only by authorised admin personnel

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We share your data only in these circumstances:

  • With your specialist: Assessment summaries and session context, with your explicit consent
  • Service providers: Payment processors, cloud hosting providers, email services — all bound by data processing agreements
  • Legal requirements: If required by court order, warrant, or applicable law
  • Safety: If we reasonably believe disclosure is necessary to prevent imminent harm

5. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on secure, encrypted servers. We use industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption, bcrypt password hashing, and regular security audits. Access to production databases is restricted to authorised personnel only.

While we take every reasonable precaution, no system is perfectly secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and enable two-factor authentication when available.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and all associated data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability: Export your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities

These rights are guaranteed under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023 and the IT (Amendment) Act. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@serenitymindspace.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal cookies necessary for platform operation: session authentication, preference storage (dark mode, language), and basic analytics. We do not use third-party advertising cookies. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though some platform features may not function correctly.

8. Children's Privacy

Serenity Mindspace is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify registered users of material changes by email at least 14 days in advance. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance.
